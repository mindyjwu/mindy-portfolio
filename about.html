About

I'm Mindy Wu, based in New York. I studied Computer Science and Data Science at NYU, and I build and deploy RAG pipelines and AI systems for Fortune 500 media and entertainment companies. On the side, I build small AI tools for problems I'm personally curious about.

This site started with my parents. They don't work in tech, and they kept asking me — separately — whether AI was going to take their jobs. I never had an answer that fit in a text message, so instead of explaining it, I started showing them: here's how you'd use it for an email you're dreading, a letter you don't understand, a conversation you've been putting off. The tools on this site grew out of that, and then out of my own curiosity.

What I keep coming back to is that the people who get the most out of AI usually aren't the ones who know the most about it. They're the ones who already have real judgment, real taste, and a real point of view — and just need a faster way to act on it. I think good tools should meet people where they are and help them do more of what they're already good at. That's the lens I build through, at work and here.
