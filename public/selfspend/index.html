What you do not have to worry about
  • We never see your bank password. You log in on your bank's page via Plaid. We get a read-only token for the one card you pick.
  • We cannot move money. Read-only means list transactions, nothing else.
  • No name, no account number, no balance. We keep the merchant line, amount and date. That is all.
  • No monthly total, ever. It is a design rule, tested in code.
  • Nothing is sold or shared. No brokers, no ads, no merchants told you visited.
  • One tap deletes everything, including revoking the bank link at Plaid. No waiting period.
  • This is a one-person beta, on one server, not a bank. Skip bank connect if that is not for you. CSV and typing work the same.
